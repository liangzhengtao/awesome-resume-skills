## Why This Collection?

Most resume advice is generic. These 12 AI skills give you **actionable frameworks, fill-in-the-blank templates, and before/after examples** for every resume format — from corporate CVs to infographic resumes to technical portfolios.

Each skill includes:
- **When to Use** — clear triggers for when each skill applies
- **Framework** — structured approach with formulas and guidelines
- **Templates** — copy-paste, fill-in-the-blank formats
- **Examples** — real before/after transformations
- **Common Mistakes** — pitfalls to avoid
- **中文版本** — Chinese version for every skill

## Quick Start

1. **Identify your category** — Are you targeting traditional, creative, or technical roles?
2. **Pick your skill** — Choose the skill that matches your target role
3. **Read the framework** — Understand the structure and principles
4. **Use the template** — Copy and fill in the blanks with your information
5. **Study the examples** — Compare before/after to refine your content
6. **Check common mistakes** — Avoid the pitfalls others fall into
