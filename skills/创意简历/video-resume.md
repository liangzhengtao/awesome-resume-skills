# Video Resume Skill

> Script writing and production guide for video resumes and elevator pitches.

## When to Use

- Applying to media, broadcasting, or communications roles
- Startups or companies that value personality and culture fit
- When explicitly requested by the employer
- As a supplement to your traditional resume
- Freelance or consulting roles where personal brand matters

**NOTE:** Video resumes should never replace a traditional written resume. Use them as a supplement when appropriate.

## Framework

### Video Resume Structure

```
[0:00 - 0:05]  HOOK — Grab attention immediately
[0:05 - 0:20]  INTRODUCTION — Who you are, what you do
[0:20 - 0:45]  VALUE — Key achievements and skills
[0:45 - 0:55]  FIT — Why this company/role
[0:55 - 1:00]  CTA — Call to action
```

### Production Quality Checklist

- [ ] Camera at eye level
- [ ] Face well-lit (natural light or ring light)
- [ ] Clean, uncluttered background
- [ ] Professional attire
- [ ] Clear audio (use external mic if possible)
- [ ] Stable camera (tripod or stable surface)
- [ ] Landscape orientation (16:9)
- [ ] 1080p minimum resolution

## Templates

### 60-Second Elevator Pitch Script

```
[HOOK — 5 seconds]
"Hi, I'm [Name], and I [attention-grabbing achievement or statement]."

[INTRODUCTION — 15 seconds]
"I'm a [title] with [X] years of experience in [field/industry].
Currently at [Company], I [brief role description]. Previously, I
[one-line previous experience highlight]."

[VALUE — 25 seconds]
"What sets me apart is [unique value proposition]. For example:

[Key achievement 1]: I [action verb] [what you did], which resulted
in [measurable outcome].

[Key achievement 2]: I also [action verb] [what you did], leading to
[measurable outcome]."

[FIT — 10 seconds]
"I'm excited about [Company] because [specific reason — their mission,
product, recent news, culture]. I believe my experience in [relevant
skill] would help your team [specific contribution]."

[CTA — 5 seconds]
"I'd love to discuss how I can contribute to [Company]. Let's connect
at [email] or [LinkedIn]. Thank you!"
```

### 3-Minute Detailed Introduction Script

```
[HOOK — 10 seconds]
[Start with a compelling statement, question, or brief story that
connects to your professional identity.]

Example: "Three years ago, I watched a patient's life get saved by
an algorithm I helped build. That's when I knew data science wasn't
just my career — it was my calling."

[INTRODUCTION — 30 seconds]
"Hello, I'm [Full Name], a [professional title] based in [Location].

I specialize in [2-3 key areas of expertise]. Over the past [X] years,
I've [high-level career summary — industries, scale of impact].

Currently, I serve as [Title] at [Company], where I [brief description
of role and key responsibilities]."

[PROFESSIONAL JOURNEY — 45 seconds]
"My career began at [First Company/Role], where I [what you learned
or accomplished].

I then moved to [Second Company], where I [growth or key achievement].

At [Current/Most Recent Company], I've been able to [major accomplishment
or responsibility], including:

• [Specific project or achievement #1 with metric]
• [Specific project or achievement #2 with metric]
• [Specific project or achievement #3 with metric]"

[SKILLS & EXPERTISE — 30 seconds]
"Technically, I bring expertise in:
• [Key technical skill or methodology]
• [Key technical skill or methodology]
• [Key technical skill or methodology]

Beyond technical skills, I'm known for [soft skill 1] and [soft skill 2].
[Colleagues/Managers often describe me as [specific compliment with evidence]."

[WHY THIS COMPANY — 30 seconds]
"I'm particularly drawn to [Company Name] because:

First, [specific reason — recent product launch, mission alignment,
industry leadership].

Second, [another reason — team culture, growth opportunity, values].

I've been following [specific company news or initiative], and I believe
my experience in [relevant area] positions me to contribute meaningfully
to [specific team or goal]."

[CALL TO ACTION — 15 seconds]
"I would welcome the opportunity to bring my [key skill 1] and
[key skill 2] to your team.

You can reach me at [email] or connect with me on LinkedIn at
[your LinkedIn URL].

Thank you for your time, and I look forward to the possibility of
contributing to [Company Name]'s continued success."
```

### Script Template: Career Changer

```
[HOOK]
"After 8 years in [Old Industry], I made the leap to [New Industry]
— and it was the best decision of my career."

[BRIDGE]
"While working as [Old Title], I discovered my passion for [New Field]
when [specific moment that triggered the change]. I realized that the
skills I'd built — [transferable skill 1], [transferable skill 2], and
[transferable skill 3] — were exactly what [New Industry] needed."

[PREPARATION]
"To make this transition, I:
• [Action 1: course, certification, bootcamp]
• [Action 2: personal project, volunteer work]
• [Action 3: networking, mentorship]"

[VALUE]
"In just [timeframe] since transitioning, I've already:
• [Achievement in new field #1]
• [Achievement in new field #2]"

[FIT + CTA]
"I'm excited to bring my unique perspective — combining [Old Industry]
expertise with [New Industry] skills — to [Company]."
```

## Examples

### Before & After: Opening Lines

**Before (boring):**
```
"Hi, my name is John Smith and I'm looking for a marketing job."
```

**After (compelling):**
```
"Last quarter, I turned a $5,000 ad budget into $150,000 in revenue.
Hi, I'm John Smith — and I make marketing dollars work harder."
```

**Before (generic):**
```
"I am a software engineer with 5 years of experience."
```

**After (specific):**
```
"I've shipped code that serves 10 million users every day.
I'm a software engineer who builds products at scale."
```

### Before & After: Achievement Description

**Before:**
```
"I worked on improving our app's performance and it got faster."
```

**After:**
```
"Our mobile app was taking 8 seconds to load — I cut that to under
1 second by rewriting the data layer in Rust, which also reduced
our server costs by 40%. That's the kind of impact I love making."
```

## Production Tips

### Lighting Setup

```
         [Window/Natural Light]
                 │
                 ▼
    ┌────────────────────────┐
    │                        │
    │   💡 Ring Light        │
    │       │                │
    │       ▼                │
    │    🧑 YOU              │
    │                        │
    │        [Clean Wall /   │
    │         Bookshelf]     │
    └────────────────────────┘
    
    Camera at eye level
    Light source in FRONT of you
    Avoid overhead lighting (creates shadows)
    Avoid backlighting (silhouette effect)
```

### Audio Setup

| Option | Cost | Quality | Best For |
|--------|------|---------|----------|
| Lavalier mic (clip-on) | $15-50 | Excellent | Talking head videos |
| USB condenser mic | $50-150 | Excellent | Desk recording |
| Phone earbuds | $0 | Good | Quick recordings |
| Built-in laptop mic | $0 | Poor | Avoid if possible |

### Background Options

1. **Clean wall** — Solid color, neutral tone (gray, white, light blue)
2. **Bookshelf** — Shows intellectual curiosity (tidy it up first)
3. **Office setup** — Professional but personal items visible
4. **Blur** — Use software background blur if your space is messy
5. **Virtual** — Only if high quality; low-quality virtual backgrounds look unprofessional

### Recording Tools

| Tool | Platform | Cost | Features |
|------|----------|------|----------|
| Zoom | All | Free | Record, basic editing |
| Loom | Web/Desktop | Free | Screen + camera, easy sharing |
| OBS Studio | All | Free | Professional recording |
| Descript | All | Paid | AI editing, transcription |
| CapCut | All | Free | Easy mobile/desktop editing |
| iMovie | Mac/iOS | Free | Simple editing |

## Common Mistakes

1. **Too long** — Keep it under 2 minutes for initial applications
2. **Reading from script** — Use bullet points, memorize key points, look at camera
3. **Poor audio** — Bad audio is worse than bad video; invest in a mic
4. **Distracting background** — Keep it clean and professional
5. **No energy** — Practice until you sound natural and enthusiastic
6. **Wrong format** — Send as MP4, H.264 codec, under 50MB
7. **No subtitles** — Add captions for accessibility and silent viewing
8. **Generic content** — Tailor each video to the specific company
9. **Filming vertically** — Always landscape (16:9) unless for TikTok/Reels

## 中文版本

### 适用场景

- 申请媒体、广播、传播类职位
- 初创公司或重视企业文化的公司
- 雇主明确要求时
- 作为传统简历的补充
- 自由职业或咨询类角色

### 中文视频简历注意事项

- **时长控制**：初投1分钟以内，详细版不超过3分钟
- **语言表达**：普通话标准，避免过多口头禅
- **着装**：正装或商务休闲，视行业而定
- **背景音乐**：一般不加，保持专业感
- **字幕**：中文字幕是必须的

### 推荐平台

- 投递方式：邮件附件或网盘链接
- 展示平台：B站、抖音（仅限创意行业）
- 工具：剪映（字幕自动生成）、OBS Studio
