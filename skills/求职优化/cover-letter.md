# Cover Letter Skill

> Writing compelling cover letters that complement your resume.

## When to Use

- When the job posting requests a cover letter
- When you want to explain career transitions or gaps
- When applying to companies that value culture and motivation
- When you have a referral and want to mention the connection
- When you're genuinely excited about the company/role

**NOTE:** Many recruiters skip cover letters. Prioritize a strong resume, but include a cover letter when it adds genuine value.

## Framework

### Cover Letter Structure

```
[PARAGRAPH 1 — HOOK] (2-3 sentences)
Why this company, why this role, what draws you in.
Reference something specific (recent news, product, mission).

[PARAGRAPH 2 — VALUE] (3-4 sentences)
Your most relevant experience and achievements.
Mirror the job description's key requirements.
Include 1-2 quantified accomplishments.

[PARAGRAPH 3 — FIT] (2-3 sentences)
Why you specifically fit this team/company culture.
Reference company values, mission, or recent work.
Show you've done your research.

[PARAGRAPH 4 — CTA] (1-2 sentences)
Express enthusiasm for next steps.
Thank them for their consideration.
```

### Opening Hook Formulas

```
FORMULA 1: Achievement + Connection
"[Your biggest relevant achievement]. I'm excited to bring this
[skill/approach] to [Company] as a [Role]."

FORMULA 2: Company Knowledge + Enthusiasm
"After [specific interaction with company — using product, reading
article, attending event], I knew I wanted to contribute to
[Company]'s [mission/goal]."

FORMULA 3: Referral Introduction
"[Referrer Name] suggested I reach out regarding the [Role] position.
Given my [X] years in [field] and [key achievement], I'm confident
I can contribute to [team/goal]."

FORMULA 4: Mission Alignment
"[Company]'s commitment to [mission/value] resonates deeply with
my own [passion/experience in area]. As a [title] with [X] years
in [field], I'm eager to help [specific company goal]."
```

```

[City, State ZIP]

[Date]

[HOOK — 2-3 sentences connecting your experience to the role.

At [Current/Recent Company], I [top achievement with metric], and
I'm eager to bring this [skill/approach] to [Company Name].

In my role as [Title] at [Company], I:
• [Achievement 1 — most relevant to job description]
• [Achievement 2 — second most relevant]
• [Achievement 3 — demonstrates additional value]

What excites me about [Company] is [specific reason — mission, product,

[area] can contribute to [Company]'s [goal/mission]. Thank you for
your consideration, and I look forward to hearing from you.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
```

PREPARATION — WHAT I'VE DONE:
To prepare for this transition, I:
• [Course, certification, or degree in new field]
• [Personal project or volunteer work in new field]
• [Networking, mentorship, or industry involvement]

VALUE — WHAT I BRING:

the chance to discuss how my diverse background can add value.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

## Examples

### Before & After: Opening Lines

**Before (generic):**
```
"I am writing to apply for the Software Engineer position at your
company. I believe I am a good fit for this role."
```

**After (specific and engaging):**
```
"After building my first open-source contribution to React's
documentation at 19, I knew I wanted to work where engineering
meets millions of users. Google's approach to developer tooling —
particularly the recent launch of Gemini API for developers —
represents exactly the kind of impact I want to make."
```

**Before (boring):**
```
"I am a marketing professional with 5 years of experience. I saw
your job posting and I am interested in applying."
```

**After (achievement-led):**
```
"Growing a SaaS startup's revenue from $2M to $12M in 18 months
taught me that great marketing is really great storytelling backed
by data. I'm excited to bring that approach to HubSpot's Content
Hub team."
```

### Company Research Methods

```
BEFORE WRITING, RESEARCH:

1. COMPANY WEBSITE
   - About page, mission, values
   - Recent blog posts, press releases
   - Product pages, case studies

2. NEWS
   - Google News search for company name
   - Recent funding, launches, leadership changes
   - Industry trends affecting the company

3. SOCIAL MEDIA
   - LinkedIn company page
   - Twitter/X — what does the team share?
   - Engineering blog (if applicable)

4. PEOPLE
   - Hiring manager's LinkedIn
   - Team members' backgrounds
   - Glassdoor reviews (take with grain of salt)

5. PRODUCTS
   - Actually use the product/service
   - Read user reviews, documentation
   - Understand the competitive landscape
```

### Matching Skills to Requirements

```
JOB POSTING:                    YOUR COVER LETTER:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
"Experience with Python         → "At Company X, I built Python-based
 and data analysis"               data pipelines analyzing 2M+ records
                                  daily, reducing report generation
                                  from 8 hours to 15 minutes."

"Strong communication           → "I presented quarterly analytics
 and stakeholder management"      findings to C-suite stakeholders,
                                  translating technical insights into
                                  actionable business recommendations."

"Experience in agile            → "I led 3-week sprint cycles for a
 development environments"        team of 6, consistently delivering
                                  features on time across 12 sprints."
```

## Common Mistakes

1. **Too long** — Keep to 250-400 words (3-4 paragraphs)
2. **Repeating your resume** — Cover letter should complement, not duplicate
3. **Generic "To Whom It May Concern"** — Find the hiring manager's name
4. **Focusing on what you want** — Focus on what you can give
5. **No specifics about the company** — Generic = trash bin
6. **Apologizing for gaps** — Address briefly and move on to strengths
7. **Forgetting to proofread** — Typos in a letter about "attention to detail"
8. **Using the same letter for every application** — Customize each one

## 中文版本

### 适用场景

- 职位描述要求提供求职信
- 需要解释职业转换或空白期
- 申请重视企业文化和动机的公司
- 有内推想要提及推荐人

### 中文求职信（自荐信）格式

```
[标题：自荐信]

尊敬的[招聘负责人]：

您好！

[第一段：介绍自己 + 申请职位 + 引起兴趣的亮点]

[第二段：核心经验 + 匹配度 + 量化成果（2-3个）]

[第三段：对公司的了解 + 文化契合度 + 具体原因]

[第四段：表达面试意愿 + 感谢]

此致
敬礼

[姓名]
[日期]
[联系方式]
```

### 中文求职信注意事项

- **称呼**：尽量找到具体人名，避免"尊敬的领导"
- **篇幅**：500-800字，不超过一页A4纸
- **语气**：自信但不自大，热情但不夸张
- **亮点前置**：最重要的成就放在最前面
- **避免套话**：不要写"给我一个机会，还你一个奇迹"
