# LinkedIn Optimization Skill

> Optimize your LinkedIn profile for recruiter visibility and professional networking.

## When to Use

- Actively job searching (LinkedIn is #1 recruiter sourcing tool)
- Building professional network and personal brand
- Transitioning to a new industry or role
- When recruiters aren't finding your profile
- At least quarterly maintenance even if not job searching

## Framework

### LinkedIn Profile Sections to Optimize

1. **Profile Photo** — Professional headshot (400×400px minimum)
2. **Banner Image** — Professional or industry-relevant (1584×396px)
3. **Headline** — 220 characters, keyword-rich
4. **About Section** — 2,600 characters max, tell your story
5. **Experience** — Similar to resume but with LinkedIn-specific formatting
6. **Skills** — 50 skills max, top 3 should match your target role
7. **Recommendations** — At least 3-5 from colleagues/managers
8. **Featured Section** — Pin best content (posts, articles, links)
9. **Education** — Degree, activities, coursework
10. **Certifications** — Industry certifications
11. **Activity** — Regular posting and engagement

### SEO for LinkedIn

LinkedIn's search algorithm considers:

| Factor | Weight | Action |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Headline keywords | High | Include target role keywords |
| Current title | High | Use industry-standard titles |
| Skills section | High | Add all relevant skills |
| About section | Medium | Naturally include keywords |
| Experience descriptions | Medium | Use keywords in achievements |
| Endorsements | Medium | Get endorsed for key skills |
| Activity/Posting | Medium | Post 2-3x per week |
| Profile completeness | Medium | Fill every section |

## Templates

### Headline Formulas

```
FORMULA 1: Title + Specialization + Value Proposition
"[Title] Specializing in [Area] | Helping [Target Audience]
[Achieve What] | [Credential]"

Examples:
"Senior Data Scientist Specializing in NLP | Helping SaaS Companies
Turn Text Data into Revenue | PhD Stanford"

"Full-Stack Engineer | Building Scalable Web Apps That Serve 10M+
Users | Ex-Meta, Ex-Stripe"

FORMULA 2: Title + Company + Keywords
"[Title] at [Company] | [Keyword 1] | [Keyword 2] | [Keyword 3]"

Examples:
"Product Manager at Google | AI/ML Products | Growth Strategy |
User Research"

FORMULA 3: Achievement-Led
"[Title] | [Impressive Stat] | [What You Do]"

Examples:
"Marketing Director | Grew Revenue 340% in 18 Months | B2B SaaS
Growth & Demand Generation"

"Software Engineer | Shipped Code Used by 500M+ People | Distributed
Systems & Cloud Infrastructure"

FORMULA 4: Career Changer
"[Target Role] | Transitioning from [Background] | [Relevant New Skill]
| [Transferable Strength]"

Examples:
"Data Analyst | Former Marketing Manager Leveraging 8 Years of
Consumer Insights | Python, SQL, Tableau"
```

### Networking Messages

**Connection Request (Cold):**
```
Hi [Name], I came across your work on [specific project/post/article]
and was impressed by [specific detail]. I'm a [your title] with
experience in [relevant area] and would love to connect. Looking
forward to learning from your insights!
```

**Connection Request (Warm — mutual connection):**
```
Hi [Name], [Mutual Connection] suggested we connect — I'm working
on [project/initiative] in [area] and your expertise in [their
area] would be invaluable. Would love to exchange ideas!
```

**Follow-up After Connecting:**
```
Thanks for connecting, [Name]! I noticed you're working on [specific
area] at [Company] — I'm particularly interested in [aspect of
their work]. Would you be open to a quick 15-minute chat about
[specific topic]? Happy to share my perspective on [area you can
add value] in return.
```

## Profile Photo Guidelines

| Aspect | Guideline |
|--------|-----------|
| Resolution | 400×400px minimum (displays at 200×200) |
| Framing | Head and shoulders, face takes 60%+ of frame |
| Background | Solid color or simple office/outdoor setting |
| Expression | Smile naturally; approachable but professional |
| Attire | Match your industry's dress code |
| Lighting | Even, front-facing light; no harsh shadows |
| Recency | Within 2 years |
| Crop | Square; face centered |

## Common Mistakes

1. **No profile photo** — Profiles with photos get 21x more views
2. **Default headline** — Using only your job title misses keyword opportunities
3. **Empty About section** — This is your chance to tell your story
4. **Not customizing URL** — linkedin.com/in/yourname is cleaner than random string
5. **No skills endorsements** — Top 3 skills should match target role
6. **Never posting/commenting** — Activity signals = more visibility
7. **Ignoring connection requests** — Grow your network strategically
8. **Copy-pasting resume** — LinkedIn allows a more conversational tone
9. **Open to Work too aggressively** — Use the LinkedIn feature, but keep your profile focused on value

## 中文版本

### 适用场景

- 积极求职时（LinkedIn是全球最大的招聘搜索工具）
- 建立职业网络和个人品牌
- 转行或转型时
- 定期维护（即使不在求职）

### LinkedIn vs 脉脉

| 维度 | LinkedIn | 脉脉 |
|------|----------|------|
| 用户群 | 外企、海归、国际化公司 | 国内互联网公司 |
| 内容语言 | 英文为主 | 中文 |
| 社交方式 | 专业克制 | 匿名+职场八卦 |
| 求职功能 | 全球职位 | 国内职位 |
| 推荐使用 | 外企、出海公司、技术圈 | 国内互联网、金融 |

### 中文LinkedIn优化要点

- **标题**：中英双语，覆盖两种搜索
- **About**：英文为主，中文简历另备
- **技能**：添加中英文技能关键词
- **推荐信**：请国际同事或上级写英文推荐
- **活跃度**：定期发英文行业见解帖子

### 脉脉优化要点

- **实名认证**：增加可信度
- **职位描述**：详细填写，使用行业术语
- **人脉拓展**：主动添加同行业人士
- **内容输出**：分享专业见解，建立影响力
