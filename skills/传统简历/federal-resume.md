# Federal Resume Skill

> Extended-format resume for U.S. government jobs (USAJobs, GS positions, military civilian roles).

## When to Use

- Applying through USAJobs.gov
- State or local government positions
- Federal contractor roles requiring detailed background
- Military transition to civilian government roles

## Framework

### Federal Resume Structure

Federal resumes are significantly longer than private-sector resumes (4-8 pages typical).

1. **Contact Information** — Full name, address, phone, email, citizenship, veteran preference
2. **Position Details** — Announcement number, grade, series
3. **Security Clearance** — Level, status, investigation date
4. **Work Experience** — Detailed (see below)
5. **Education** — Degrees with credit hours if applicable
6. **Certifications & Licenses**
7. **Volunteer Experience**
8. **Language Skills**
9. **References** — 3-5 professional references with full contact info

### Work Experience Format (Critical)

Each position must include:
- Job title, series, and grade (if federal)
- Agency/Organization name and location
- Start and end dates (MM/YYYY)
- Hours per week
- Supervisor name, phone, and whether they may be contacted
- Detailed duties (500-1000 words per position)
- Accomplishments with metrics
- Relevant KSAs (Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities) demonstrated

### KSAs (Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities)

KSAs are the core of federal hiring. Each vacancy announcement lists required competencies.

**Structure:**
```
KSA: [Competency Name]
Situation: [Describe the context]
Task: [What you needed to do]
Action: [Steps you took]
Result: [Measurable outcome]
```

### KSA Response Template

```
KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS, AND ABILITIES (KSAs)

KSA #1: [Competency from announcement]
Example: "Knowledge of data analysis and visualization tools"

I have extensive experience applying data analysis methodologies in federal
settings. While serving as [Title] at [Agency] from [Date] to [Date]:

SITUATION: The agency needed to analyze [X] records to identify [issue].

TASK: I was responsible for developing a comprehensive analysis framework to
process and visualize [data type] for senior leadership decision-making.

ACTION: I utilized [specific tools — Python, Tableau, SQL] to:
1. Cleaned and normalized [X] data points from [X] source systems
2. Built automated ETL pipelines reducing manual processing by [X] hours/week
3. Created interactive dashboards with [X] views for [X] stakeholder groups
4. Established data quality protocols that reduced errors by [X]%

RESULT: My analysis identified [specific finding], enabling leadership to
[action taken]. This resulted in [quantified outcome — cost savings, improved
efficiency, policy change]. The framework I developed was adopted agency-wide
and continues to serve [X] program offices.

---

KSA #2: [Next competency]
[Same detailed STAR format]
```

### Federal Resume Checklist

```
□ Contact information complete (including full mailing address)
□ Citizenship status stated
□ Veterans' preference indicated
□ Security clearance details included
□ Announcement number referenced
□ All work experience with MM/YYYY dates
□ Hours per week for each position
□ Supervisor information for each position
□ KSAs addressed in experience descriptions
□ Education with credit hours (if no degree)
□ Relevant certifications included
□ Volunteer experience included
□ 3-5 references with full contact info
□ Resume is 4-8 pages
□ Keywords from announcement incorporated
```

## Common Mistakes

1. **Using a 1-page private-sector resume** — Federal resumes need 4-8 pages
2. **Omitting hours per week** — Required for every position
3. **Leaving out supervisor info** — Verify requirement for each announcement
4. **Ignoring KSAs** — Map every KSA to specific experience
5. **Vague accomplishments** — Include numbers, percentages, dollar amounts
6. **Missing citizenship/veteran status** — Always include
7. **Applying after deadline** — USAJobs closes at 11:59 PM ET on the deadline date
8. **Not tailoring to announcement** — Mirror the language of the posting

## 中文版本

### 适用场景

- 申请美国政府职位（USAJobs系统）
- 州或地方政府岗位
- 需要详细背景调查的联邦承包商职位
- 军人转型文职政府岗位

### 中文简历差异

中国公务员简历与美国联邦简历差异较大：
- 中国：通常1-2页，格式统一，由组织部门制定模板
- 美国联邦：4-8页，详细到每小时工作经历
- 核心区别：美国强调KSA（知识、技能、能力）的逐一映射

### 关键要素

- 简历长度：4-8页（远长于私营部门）
- 必须包含：公民身份、退伍军人优先权、安全许可
- 每段经历需要：确切日期、每周工时、主管联系方式
- KSA回应是联邦简历的核心
