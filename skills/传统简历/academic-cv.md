# Academic CV Skill

> Complete CV for researchers, professors, postdocs, and PhD candidates.

## When to Use

- Applying for faculty positions (tenure-track, adjunct, lecturer)
- Postdoc applications and research fellowships
- Grant applications requiring CV
- Academic job market (R1, R2, liberal arts colleges)

## Framework

### Academic CV Sections (in order)

1. **Header** — Name, institutional affiliation, email, ORCID, Google Scholar link
2. **Education** — PhD, Master's, Bachelor's (include dissertation title & advisor)
3. **Academic Appointments** — Current and previous positions
4. **Research Interests** — 2-4 lines describing focus areas
5. **Publications** — Peer-reviewed articles, grouped by type
6. **Grants & Fellowships** — Funded projects with amounts
7. **Conference Presentations** — Talks, posters, invited lectures
8. **Teaching Experience** — Courses taught, mentoring
9. **Service** — Department, university, professional community
10. **Awards & Honors** — Fellowships, prizes, distinctions
11. **Skills** — Languages, software, lab techniques (if relevant)
12. **References** — 3-5 names with full contact info

### Length

No page limit. CV should be complete.
- PhD student / Postdoc: 3-6 pages
- Assistant Professor: 6-12 pages
- Associate/Full Professor: 10-25+ pages

## Publication Formatting Styles

### APA 7th Edition
```
Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of article. Title of Periodical, Volume(Issue), Pages. https://doi.org/xxxxx
```

### IEEE
```
A. A. Author and B. B. Author, "Title of article," Title of Periodical, vol. X, no. X, pp. XX–XX, Month Year, doi: xxxxx.
```

### Chicago
```
Author, A. A., and B. B. Author. "Title of Article." Title of Periodical Volume, no. Issue (Year): Pages. https://doi.org/xxxxx.
```

### Grant Formatting
```
[Year–Year] "[Project Title]." [Funding Agency, Program Name].
         $[Total Amount]. Role: [PI/Co-PI/Senior Personnel].
         Status: [Funded/Pending/Completed].
```

## Examples

### Before & After: Publication Entry

**Before (incomplete):**
```
Smith, J. (2023). Machine learning in healthcare. Nature Medicine.
```

**After (proper APA):**
```
Smith, J. A., Johnson, M. R., & Williams, K. L. (2023). A systematic
review of machine learning applications in early-stage cancer diagnosis.
Nature Medicine, 29(4), 812–824. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-023-xxxxx
```

### Before & After: Research Description

**Before:**
```
Research: I study machine learning and biology.
```

**After:**
```
RESEARCH INTERESTS
My research program sits at the intersection of computational biology and
machine learning, with three primary thrusts: (1) developing novel deep
learning architectures for protein structure prediction, (2) applying
Bayesian methods to single-cell RNA sequencing data, and (3) building
interpretable models for clinical decision support. This work has been
supported by $1.2M in external funding from NIH and NSF.
```

## Common Mistakes

1. **Including irrelevant work experience** — Leave off non-academic jobs unless they demonstrate transferable skills
2. **No ORCID** — Get one; it's becoming standard for publication tracking
3. **Inconsistent citation format** — Pick one style and stick with it
4. **Listing "in preparation" papers that never materialize** — Only include if actively writing
5. **Missing dissertation title and advisor** — Essential for academic CVs
6. **No Google Scholar link** — Hiring committees will look you up anyway
7. **Forgetting service** — Shows you're a good colleague and citizen

## 中文版本

### 适用场景

- 申请大学教职（终身轨、讲师、兼职教授）
- 博士后申请和研究奖学金
- 基金申请需要个人简历
- 学术就业市场

### 中文学术简历特殊要求

- **发表论文**：按国内学位要求格式，中文论文在前，英文论文在后
- **课题项目**：国家自然科学基金、省部级项目单独列出
- **学术兼职**：学会会员、期刊编委、审稿人
- **研究生指导**：列出已毕业和在读研究生

### 格式要点

- 无页数限制，但内容应真实、完整
- 论文格式遵循目标期刊/学科规范
- 硕博论文需注明导师姓名和论文题目
- 国内申请通常需要附代表性论著全文
